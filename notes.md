## Install

1. Download `Bitamp-1.0.0.zip`, unzip it, and move **Bitamp** to Applications.
2. Bitamp isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS will say it can't check it for malware. Click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the note about Bitamp. You only need to do this once.

   Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Bitamp.app`

Runs on macOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
